## Consequences of Groups

## 1. Functional Behaviour
These are **positive outcomes** of groups.

Your notes list:

**Information Exchange**

- Members share knowledge and information.
- Different members bring different perspectives.

**Diversity in Workforce**

- People from different backgrounds can contribute different ideas and viewpoints.

**Composition of Groups**

- Who is included in the group affects how the group functions.

So remember:

> **Functional = Information Exchange + Diversity + Composition**

---

### 2. Dysfunctional Behaviour ❌

These are **negative effects** that can arise in groups.

Your PDF specifically lists:

**Group Polarization**

**Groupthink**

#### Group Polarization ⭐

This means that after group discussion, members can move toward a **more extreme version of the position they initially held**.

Example:

Before discussion:

> “Maybe we should increase the project budget slightly.”

After everyone discusses:

> “Let's massively increase the budget!”

The group's final position becomes **more extreme**.

#### Groupthink ⭐⭐⭐

We already covered this:

> Group members become so focused on agreement and conformity that they fail to properly consider alternatives.

Your notes specifically associate groupthink with conditions such as **high cohesiveness, isolation from outsiders, pressure to conform, and a leader promoting a preferred solution.****
