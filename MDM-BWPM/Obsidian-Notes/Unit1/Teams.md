## What is a Team?
A collection of 3 or more individuals who
interact intensively to provide an organizational
product, plan, decision or service

## Characteristics

### 1. Interdependence
- Members depend on one another's 
  - Assistance
  - Expertise
  - Opinions
### 2. Power Differentiation 
- team tries to maintain equity and avoid unnecessary dominance.
### 3. Social Distance 
can be reduced through:
- empathy 
- casual interaction 
- expressing liking
- common views
### 4. Conflict Management 
Important tactics include: 
- Collabrations. 
- Understanding other's views
- compromise
- Non- threatening communication.

## Types of Teams
◦ **Work teams**
	◦ Members manage themselves, assign jobs, plan and schedule work,
	make work-related decisions, solve work-related problems
	◦ Employees cross trained
	◦ Responsible for their own productivity
◦ **Parallel teams**
	◦ Cross functional teams that have representatives from various
	departments
	◦ Essential to identify clear purpose, receive support and establish trust
◦ **Project teams**
	◦ Created to produce onetime outputs
	◦ Dismantled once the goal is accomplished
◦ **Management teams**
	◦ Coordinate, manage, advise and direct teams and employees
	◦ Responsible for providing general direction and assistance


## Why Teams Don't Always Work?
### 1. Excessive meeting requirements

Too many meetings can:

- Waste time
- Cause frequent interruptions
- Make discussions unnecessarily long

### 2. Lack of empowerment

Team members may not have enough authority to make decisions.

### 3. Lack of authority

Managers may worry about:

- Quality of work
- Team members overstepping boundaries

### 4. Team members reject empowerment

Sometimes employees themselves may not want the additional responsibility.

### 5. Lack of skill

Team members may lack the necessary:

- Skills
- Expertise
- Problem-solving ability

### 6. Distrust of the team process

Members or managers may not trust the team-based approach.

### 7. Managers unwilling to give authority

Managers may find it difficult to delegate authority and therefore need training.

### 8. Lack of receptivity among team members

Low:

- Job satisfaction
- Organizational commitment

can make members less receptive to teamwork.

### 9. Nature of work

Not every type of work is suitable for a team.

### 10. Unclear objectives

A team needs clarity about:

- **Why** the team is formed
- **What** is expected
- **When** it should finish/dismantle
