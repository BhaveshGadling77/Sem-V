## What is Group?
- Any number of people who interact with each other and perceive themselves to be a group.
- People who interact with each other and consider themselves as a unit. 
### Characteristics
- Members see themselves as a **UNIT**
- Group provides rewards for all.
- corresponding effects. 
- Common Goals.

## Why Do people join Group?
- Assignments - **Common task**
- Physical Proximity - People are nearby
- Affiliation - need to belong
- Identification - association with a cause
- Emotional Support
- Assistance or Help
- Common Goals and Interest. 

## Difference between Groups and Teams
## Teams 
An interdependent collections of individuals who work together toward a common goal and share responsibility For specific outcomes;
### Group 
- More generic in nature.
- Members work together 

### Teams 
- Mostly Created in an organizational setup.
- Members perform interdependent tasks.
- Members work toward a common goal.
- They share responsibility for specific outcome.

## Factors Affecting Group Performance 

## 1. Group Cohesiveness 
- Cohesive means how strongly group members are connected and attracted to the group.
- Factors associated with cohesiveness.
- Homegeneity
- Stability of membership
- Isolation 
- Group size.

## 2. Group Ability & Confidence 
- the overall Ability and confidence of the group members affects performance. 
## 3. Personality Of Members
- Different Personalities can affect interactions and perfomance. 
## 4. Communication Structure 
- How communication flows within the group affects perfomance. 
## 5. Group Roles
- Task Oriented -> These roles focus on **getting the group's work/task completed**.
- Social Oriented. -> These roles focus on **maintaining good relationships, cooperation, and morale within the group**.

## Presence of Other 

### 1. Social inhibition
- tendency for an individual's performance to decline when in the presence of others

### 2. Audience Effect
- Change in performance cause purely by being observed by passive onlookers.
- Impact:
  -  it can increase performance or decrease also.
### 3. Coaction 
- change in performance caused by performing task alongside other people who are doing the exact same task but with direct **Competition** or **Interaction**.
- example: running fast when jogging next to someone.
### 4. Social Loafing
- phenomenon where individuals exert less physical or mental effort when working collectively in a group than when working alone.
- in a tug of war, individuals pull with significantly less force per person than when pulling rope indivisually against a scale.
### 5. Free Rider Effect 
- A specific type of social loafing where a group member intentionally contributes little to no effort because they realize the group can complete task without them. But they will still share in the reward / success. 

### 6. Sucker Effect
- A psychological reaction where productive group member reduce their own effort to avoid being taken advantages by free riders.
- A team member intentionally slowing down their work or doing only their bare minimum share after noticing that two teammates haven't submitted any of their assigned parts.


## Group Think 
- occurs when the group becomes so cohesive and pressured to agree that members stop critically evaluating alternatives.
- **Reasons**: 
  - Is cohesive
  - is insulated from qualified outsiders.
  - has illusion of invulnerability / infallibility 
  - believes it is morally superior to adversaries.
  - is under great pressure to conform.

### Solution to Group Think
- 1. Leader should **promote Open discussion**
- 2. Create a **subgroups** to encourage disagreement.
- 3. Avoid Suppressing alternative opinions.
